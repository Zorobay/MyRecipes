---
title: Godfather Sour
description:
  Rich almondy amaretto sweetens and flavours this Whisky Sour.
ingredients:
    - title:
    - steps:
      - 30 ml Blended Scotch whisky
      - 30 ml Amaretto liqueur
      - 30 ml Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)
      - 7.5 ml Rich sugar syrup (2 sugar to 1 water)
      - 15 ml Egg white (pasteurised), or vegan egg white alternative, or 3 dashes cocktail foamer
      - Orange slice (for garnish)
instructions:
  - title:
    steps:
    - Pre-chill an old-fashioned glass and prepare an orange slice for garnish.
    - Shake all ingredients with ice.
    - Strain back into shaker.
    - Dry shake (without ice).
    - Fine strain into ice-filled glass.
    - Garnish with orange slice.
