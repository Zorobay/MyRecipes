---
title: 100 Year Old Cigar
description:
  Stirred down and spirit-forward aged rum with Bénédictine, Cynar and a touch of peated whisky in an absinthe-rinsed glass. Cigar-like and suited to late-night sipping.
ingredients:
  - title:
    steps:
    - 2.5 ml Absinthe (to rinse the glass)
    - 50 ml Caribbean blended rum, aged 6-10 years
    - 15 ml Bénédictine D.O.M. liqueur
    - 15 ml Cynar or other carciofo amaro
    - 5 ml Peated single malt whisky
    - 1 dash Angostura Aromatic Bitters
instructions:
  - title:
    steps:
    - Pre-chill a coupe glass.
    - Rinse the chilled glass with absinthe (swirl around to coat and discard excess) and set to one side.
    - Stir the other ingredients with ice.
    - Strain into the absinthe-rinsed glass.
    - Serve without garnish.
