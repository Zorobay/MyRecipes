---
title: Chicken On The Rocks
description:
  Halal cart-style chicken and rice with white sauce. Tried it first in Waterloo, Canada and fell in love with this fast food!
ingredients:
- title: Chicken
  steps:
  - 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  - 1 tbsp chopped fresh oregano
  - 1/2 tsp ground coriander seed
  - 3 garlic cloves, roughly chopped (about 1 1/2 tbsp)
  - 1/4 cup light olive oil
  - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, trimmed of excess fat (6 to 8 thighs)
  - 1 tbsp vegetable or canola oil
- title: Rice
  steps:
  - 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  - 1/2 tsp turmeric
  - 1/4 tsp ground cumin
  - 1 1/2 cups long-grain or Basmati rice
  - 2 1/2 cups chicken broth
  - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- title: Sauce
  steps:
  - 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  - 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
  - 1 tbsp sugar
  - 2 tbsp white vinegar
  - 1 tsp lemon juice
  - 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- title: To serve
  steps:
  - 1 head iceberg lettuce, shredded
  - 1 large tomato, cut into wedges
  - Fluffy pocketless pita bread, brushed in butter, lightly toasted, and cut into 1 x 3-inch strips
  - Harissa-style hot sauce, for serving
instructions:
- title: Chicken
  steps:
  - Combine the lemon juice, oregano, coriander, garlic, and olive oil in a blender. Blend until smooth. Season with salt and pepper. Place the chicken in a 1-gallon zipper-lock bag and add half of the marinade (reserve the rest in the fridge). Turn to coat, seal, and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour and up to 4 hours, turning occasionally.
  - Remove the chicken from the bag and pat dry with paper towels. Season with salt and pepper, going heavy on the pepper. Heat the oil in a 12-inch heavy-bottomed cast iron or stainless-steel skillet over medium-high heat until lightly smoking. Add the chicken and cook without disturbing until lightly browned on the first side, about 4 minutes. Flip and reduce heat to medium; cook until the chicken reaches 165°F in the center, about 6 minutes longer. Transfer to a cutting board and let cool 5 minutes.
  - Roughly chop the chicken into 1/2- to 1/4-inch chunks. Transfer to a medium bowl, add the remaining marinade, cover loosely, and refrigerate while you cook the rice and make the sauce.
- title: Rice
  steps:
  - Melt the butter over medium heat in a large Dutch oven. Add the turmeric and cumin and cook until fragrant but not browned, about 1 minute. Add the rice and stir to coat; cook, stirring frequently, until lightly toasted, about 4 minutes. Add the chicken broth and season with salt and pepper. Raise heat to high and bring to a boil. Cover, reduce to a simmer, and cook for 15 minutes without disturbing. Remove from heat and let rest until the liquid is fully absorbed and the rice is tender, about 15 minutes.
- title: Sauce
  steps:
  - In a small bowl, combine the mayonnaise, yogurt, sugar, vinegar, lemon juice, parsley, and 2 tsp black pepper. Whisk to combine and season to taste with salt.
- title: To serve
  steps:
  - Return the chicken (with marinade and juices) to the skillet. Cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until heated through. Divide the rice, lettuce, tomato, and toasted pita among plates. Pile the chicken on top of the rice. Top with white sauce and hot sauce. Serve immediately, passing extra sauce at the table.

yield: 4 to 6 servings
category: meat
total_time: 15 min prep + 40 min cooking + 1h 20min marinating/cooling

note: Do not marinate the chicken longer than 4 hours or it'll turn mushy. If you need to delay cooking, remove the chicken from the marinade, pat dry, and refrigerate until ready to cook.
