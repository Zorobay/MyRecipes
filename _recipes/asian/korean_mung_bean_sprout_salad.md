---
title: Mung Bean Sprout Side Dish (Sukjunamul-muchim)
description:
  A traditional Korean side dish of blanched mung bean sprouts tossed with garlic, fish sauce, sesame oil, and sesame seeds. Serve warm or cold; keeps well refrigerated for a few days.
ingredients:
- title:
  steps:
  - 1 pound mung bean sprouts
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 2 teaspoons fish sauce (omit and use 1½ teaspoons kosher salt total for a vegetarian version)
  - ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  - 2 green onions, chopped
  - 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  - 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds
instructions:
- title:
  steps:
  - Bring a large pot of water to a boil and blanch the sprouts for 1 minute, stirring with a wooden spoon.
  - Strain and rinse in cold water multiple times, shaking to remove any loose skins, then drain well in a basket.
  - In a bowl, combine the garlic, green onion, fish sauce, kosher salt, toasted sesame oil, and sesame seeds.
  - Add the sprouts and mix by hand to season evenly.
  - Transfer to a serving bowl or plate. Serve warm or cold. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days.
